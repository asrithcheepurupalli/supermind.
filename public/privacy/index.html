Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

supermind is a local-first second brain: your notes, links and files live on your device. This page explains, in plain words, what supermind.ink does with data. The short version is that we do not have your notes, and we have no way to get them.

What we collect

Your notebook stays on your device. We do not run accounts or a server that stores it.

  • Your notes, links, files and settings: saved in your browser on your device. They are never sent to us.
  • The name and email you enter when you set up: kept in your browser on your device only. They are not sent to us and no account is created.
  • Link previews: when you save a link, your browser asks our small preview function to read the page's public preview (its title, image and description). Only the link being saved is sent. We do not store it or log it, and your notes are never sent. For YouTube and Vimeo links, your browser asks those services' own oEmbed endpoints directly, so they can see the link and your IP address.
  • Email: if you write to us, we receive your message and your address.

What we do not do

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not run analytics on this site, we do not build profiles and we do not sell or rent anyone's details. Your notebook is never uploaded.

What the site stores in your browser

supermind keeps your notebook in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB. If you choose the encrypted vault, it is encrypted with AES-256 using your passphrase before it is saved. A service worker also saves the app itself so it works offline. You can clear all of it in your browser's site settings, and doing so deletes your notebook from this device.

Who handles it for us

A few providers process data so the site works. They act on our instructions:

  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any host it processes standard request data (IP address, browser, pages requested) in server logs. Its servers can be outside India.
  • Vercel hosts the website and runs the link preview function. Like any host it processes standard request data (IP address, browser, the address being previewed) in short-lived server logs. Its servers can be outside India.
  • YouTube and Vimeo answer link previews for their own videos, as described above. The page behind any other link you save is fetched by our preview function.
  • The typefaces are served from our own address, so no font provider sees your visit.

How long we keep it

We keep nothing of your notebook, so there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Messages you email us stay in our mailbox for as long as we need them to reply. Ask and we will delete them.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it, by writing to thebrain@made-by-ac.com. We reply ourselves. If you are in a country with data protection rights of its own, those apply too and we will honour them.

Changes and contact

If what this site does with data changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Questions, requests and complaints go to thebrain@made-by-ac.com.
